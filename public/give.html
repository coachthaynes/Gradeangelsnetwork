Become a Gift Angel

Give a teacher their evening back.

Your gift pays a vetted Grade Angel to grade a stack of papers, so a teacher gets hours back without paying for it themselves.

How your gift is used

  1. It goes to a gift balanceGifts to the community fund are shared with teachers who need help most.
  2. A teacher posts grading workA background checked Grade Angel grades it with the teacher's own key and rubric.
  3. Your gift pays for it80% goes to the Grade Angel who did the grading and 20% keeps the network running.

Your gift

You pay securely through Stripe. Grade Angels Network is a for profit company, so gifts are not tax deductible. Gifts cannot be refunded once a teacher has used them.
