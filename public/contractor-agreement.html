For Grade Angels

Independent Contractor Agreement

Version of September 24, 2026. You sign this in step 3 of Grade Angel setup.

This Independent Contractor Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between The Grade Angels Network Corporation, a Florida corporation ("Grade Angels"), and the individual who signs it during Grade Angel setup, using the name and address they gave Grade Angels (the "Grader"). The Recitals below are part of this Agreement.

Recitals

Grade Angels Network is an online platform that gives teachers, education professionals, and parents a number of benefits, including grading of assignments. The Grader has the education, knowledge, and training to grade in certain subject areas. Grade Angels wishes to engage the Grader on the terms below, and the Grader agrees to provide those services. In consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows.

I. Grading services

  1. The Grader agrees to grade assignment projects that teachers or other education professionals ("Teachers") post on Grade Angels Network, which Grade Angels offers to the Grader from time to time (each, a "Project") and which the Grader accepts (the "Services"). The Grader may accept as many or as few Projects as they choose. Once a Project is accepted, the Grader agrees to grade all of it, to the best of their ability.
  2. The Grader agrees to provide the Services in a professional, timely, and competent manner, meeting every due time, following each Project's instructions, and following the Rules in Exhibit A. Grade Angels may update the Rules from time to time. The Grader always uses their own education, training, and judgment in performing the Services.
  3. The Grader provides the Services as needed and when requests fit the Grader's schedule, and provides their own supplies and equipment. Grade Angels does not reimburse any expenses, including gas, mileage, books, subscriptions, continuing education, parking, printing, computers or other devices, writing instruments, or paper.
  4. If an emergency keeps the Grader from finishing a Project as instructed, the Grader must hand the Project back on the platform right away and tell Grade Angels.
  5. The Grader communicates with Teachers only through the Grade Angels platform messaging. The Grader will not give personal information to a Teacher or ask a Teacher for theirs. All communications between Teacher and Grader stay anonymous.
  6. The Grader is fully responsible for understanding each Project and the instructions, key, and rubric the Teacher provides. If anything is unclear, the Grader must ask the Teacher in the platform chat and confirm their understanding before grading.

II. Ownership of intellectual property

All rights in the concepts, materials, processes, methods, designs, analyses, trademarks, service marks, writings, drawings, and similar or derivative works used by Grade Angels to market, provide, and perform the Services belong to Grade Angels or its licensors, and may not be copied, reproduced, resold, published, transferred, or distributed without Grade Angels' written consent.

"Creations" means any works the Grader creates in connection with the Services, including written feedback, comments, marks, compilations, images, and documents.

  1. Works made for hire. All Creations are works made for hire for Grade Angels, which owns all copyrights and other intellectual property rights in them. The Grader will not use the Creations in any way that benefits third parties.
  2. Assignment. If any Creations are found not to be works made for hire, the Grader assigns, and agrees to assign in the future, all rights in them to Grade Angels.
  3. License. If that assignment is found ineffective, the Grader grants Grade Angels a perpetual, fully paid, worldwide, irrevocable, and exclusive license to the Creations.
  4. Teachers' use. Grade Angels lets each Teacher use the graded work returned to them for their own classes.

III. Representations of the Grader

The Grader represents and warrants that:

  1. The Grader is qualified to provide the Services because they are a current, retired, or aspiring teacher, intern, or substitute teacher, and hold any teaching certification required by the state where the Services are performed. An aspiring teacher must have completed at least 75% of their undergraduate coursework.
  2. All information the Grader gave Grade Angels about their education (including certification, degrees, specialties, grade point average, test scores, and coursework) and employment history is accurate, and the Grader has never been suspended or put on probation by any school or employer, except as fully disclosed to Grade Angels in writing.
  3. The Grader has never been convicted of a crime, except as fully disclosed to Grade Angels in writing.
  4. The Grader has given true and accurate information so Grade Angels can complete a background check, and that check must come back clear before the Grader may accept work.

IV. Covenants and conduct

  1. Performance is measured by feedback from Teachers. If performance is not satisfactory, Grade Angels may end this Agreement in its sole discretion.
  2. The Grader will be courteous, professional, honest, and responsible with Teachers at all times, and guided by fairness, non discrimination, and freedom from sexual or any other harassment.
  3. The Grader will have no direct contact with Teachers except through the Grade Angels messaging, which Grade Angels may review. Grade Angels is not responsible for what others post on the platform.
  4. The Grader is bound by the Terms of Use. If they conflict with this Agreement, this Agreement controls.
  5. The Grader may never use, or be under the influence of, drugs or alcohol while performing the Services or in any way that would affect them.

V. Pay

  1. Grade Angels pays the Grader the Service Fees in Exhibit B. Grade Angels may change the Service Fees with notice before the change applies. The Service Fees for a Project are paid once the Grader has completed it and submitted it to the Teacher as instructed.
  2. The Grader alone is responsible for all taxes on what they earn. No Social Security, federal unemployment, or local, state, or federal income taxes are withheld. Grade Angels may issue the Grader a 1099 tax form.

VI. Goodwill

  1. All Teachers who use the Services are clients of Grade Angels, not of any Grader, and all goodwill with Teachers belongs to Grade Angels.
  2. All records about Grade Angels Projects, and the information in them (including names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, payment and billing records), all amounts owed for Services to Teachers, and all rights to reviews and referrals from those Teachers belong to Grade Angels, not to the Grader.

VII. Independent contractor status

  1. The Grader provides the Services as an independent contractor, not an employee. Grade Angels does not control the methods the Grader uses, and this Agreement creates no employer and employee or principal and agent relationship. The Grader is not entitled to any employee benefits.
  2. Grade Angels may engage other Graders for the same work, and the Grader may work for themselves or others.

VIII. Compliance with laws

The Grader will follow all federal and state laws that apply to the Services, deal honestly with Grade Angels, and protect the privacy and confidentiality of all student information. If the Grader is also employed by a school, college, or school board, the Grader may not accept Projects from teachers in the same district as their workplace without that workplace's written approval. Where it applies, the Grader will comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (20 U.S.C. § 1232g; 34 CFR Part 99) and avoid any conflict, or appearance of impropriety, with their duties under it.

IX. Confidentiality

  1. What is confidential. "Confidential Information" includes (a) the business matters of Grade Angels, such as its methods, procedures, access to its website and materials, pricing, payments to Graders, intellectual property, data, vendors, sales data, marketing plans, trade secrets, software, and financial information, and (b) all information about Teachers and their students, including grades, records, names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and payment and billing records.
  2. What is not. Information that is or becomes public through no action of the Grader; that the Grader can show they knew before receiving it from Grade Angels; that someone else lawfully shares with the Grader; or that the law requires to be disclosed, if the Grader gives Grade Angels at least thirty (30) days written notice first so Grade Angels can act to prevent or limit it.
  3. How it is handled. Confidential Information belongs to the Teachers, Grade Angels, or both. The Grader will keep it in strict confidence forever, will not share it or use it for any purpose other than the Services without Grade Angels' written consent, and will not copy or change it without that consent. This continues after this Agreement ends.

X. No soliciting

During this Agreement and for two (2) years after it ends, the Grader will not solicit, deter, or try to divert or take away any Teacher who uses Grade Angels, and will not solicit, employ, or contract with Grade Angels' other Graders, contractors, administrators, or employees.

XI. Indemnification

The Grader will indemnify and hold harmless Grade Angels and its employees, officers, contractors, affiliates, agents, and representatives from all liability, losses, costs, damages, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees, court costs, and appeal costs) arising from (i) anything the Grader does or fails to do during this Agreement, (ii) the Services the Grader provides, and (iii) the Grader's duty to pay taxes on what they earn.

XII. Ending this Agreement

Either party may end this Agreement for any reason with thirty (30) days written notice. Grade Angels may end it immediately and without notice if: (1) the Grader breaks this Agreement or a representation becomes untrue; (2) the Grader's review rating falls below four stars; (3) the Grader communicates with a Teacher outside the Grade Angels messaging or exchanges personal information with a Teacher; (4) the Grader accepts payment for Services outside Grade Angels' payment process; (5) the Grader cannot complete a Project for any reason; or (6) the Grader's conduct, in Grade Angels' sole opinion, could bring negative attention or publicity to Grade Angels. When this Agreement ends, the Grader returns any Grade Angels property and deletes all student work within three (3) business days.

XIII. Notices

Notices to Grade Angels go by certified or registered mail to its address on file with the State of Florida, and are effective on the date mailed. Notices to the Grader go to the name and address the Grader gave Grade Angels during setup or on their tax form, or by email to the address on their account.

XIV. Miscellaneous

  1. Law and venue. Florida law governs this Agreement, regardless of its choice of law rules, and Clay County, Florida is the only venue for any suit or dispute resolution about it.
  2. Dispute resolution. Before starting legal action, the Grader will bring any dispute to the Grade Angels Dispute Resolution Team, the people Grade Angels names from time to time for this purpose, and meet with them to try in good faith to resolve it. If they cannot, both parties will take part in at least four hours of mediation with a mediator both agree on, unless resolved sooner, and share its cost equally. The mediator cannot force a decision.
  3. No assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent.
  4. Entire agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement about its subject and replaces all earlier discussions. It may be changed only in writing accepted by both parties; the Grader accepts an updated version by signing it on the platform.
  5. No third party beneficiaries. Nothing in this Agreement gives rights to anyone other than the parties and their permitted successors.
  6. Severability. If a court finds any part invalid, the rest stays in effect.
  7. Parties bound. This Agreement binds the parties and their heirs, executors, administrators, successors, and assigns, who will sign any further documents needed to carry it out.
  8. Captions. Headings are for convenience only.
  9. Attorneys' fees. The losing party repays the winning party's reasonable costs and attorneys' fees for enforcing this Agreement, whether or not a proceeding is brought, including at trial, arbitration, or appeal.
  10. Waiver. Not enforcing a provision does not waive it or any other provision.

Exhibit A: Rules for Grade Angels

  1. Accept only Projects you can finish well by their due time, in subjects and grade levels you know.
  2. Grade with the Teacher's own key and rubric. Ask questions in the platform chat before guessing.
  3. If you cannot finish, hand the Project back on the platform as early as possible so another Grade Angel can take it.
  4. Keep feedback kind, clear, and appropriate for the students' age.
  5. Never save, share, post, or photograph student work except to grade it on the platform. Delete any downloaded files once your grading is submitted.
  6. If you use any software or AI tool to help you grade, never give it student names or other identifying information. You remain responsible for every mark and comment.
  7. Keep all contact with Teachers in the platform chat, and never share or ask for personal contact details.
  8. Keep your payout details and profile current, and tell Grade Angels right away if anything in your background check or qualifications changes.

Exhibit B: Service Fees

  1. Each Teacher sets a price per page for their Project. The Grader earns 80% of the Project's total price (price per page times pages). Grade Angels keeps 20% as its platform fee. The Grader sees what they will earn before accepting.
  2. Service Fees are sent by electronic deposit to the Grader's connected payout account when the Teacher marks the Project complete, and in any case within 30 days of completion, unless payout setup is unfinished or the Project is in dispute. Held payouts are sent automatically once the Grader finishes payout setup.
  3. Service Fees are paid only through Grade Angels. The Grader may not accept any payment from a Teacher directly.
  4. Teachers also pay Grade Angels a separate service fee. It does not reduce the Grader's Service Fees.
  5. Service Fees, rates, and the Grade Angel Pro price are subject to change with notice before the change applies. A change never applies to a Project already accepted.

Exhibit C: Grade Angel Pro (optional)

  1. Choosing Pro. Grade Angel Pro is optional. A Grader joins by agreeing to these terms on their dashboard, and may cancel at any time; Pro then ends right away.
  2. Price and free trial. Pro costs $10 a month. A Grader's first time joining includes a 7 day free trial with no charge.
  3. How it is paid. The monthly price is taken out of the Grader's payouts, only in calendar months in which the Grader is paid. If a month's payouts are less than the price, only the amount paid out is taken. No card is charged, and nothing is owed for a month without payouts.
  4. Benefits. While Pro is active at the time a payout is sent, the Grader earns 90% of that Project's total price instead of 80%. Pro members also see new open Projects for the first 60 minutes after they are posted before other Graders, and show a Pro badge and are listed first when Teachers choose a Grader. Pro does not guarantee any amount of work.
  5. Changes. Grade Angels may change Pro's price or benefits with notice before the change applies, and the Grader may cancel instead.
