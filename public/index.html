How it works

  1. Post the workA teacher uploads the assignment with the subject, grade level, page count, and rate.
  2. A Grade Angel accepts itA vetted Grade Angel picks it up and the teacher pays securely through Stripe.
  3. Graded work comes backThe Grade Angel uploads the finished work for the teacher to review.
  4. Mark it completeOnce the teacher marks it complete, the Grade Angel is paid out.
