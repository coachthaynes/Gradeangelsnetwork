Trust and safety

How we vet every Grade Angel

Handing off student work is a big step. Here is what every Grade Angel completes before they can accept a single assignment.

1
Identity and experience on file

Every Grade Angel gives us their legal name, contact details, education, teaching certificate, and years in education.

2
Background check

A professional background check that must come back clear before any student work is shared.

3
Signed confidentiality agreement

Student work and names stay private, are used only for grading, and downloaded files are deleted once grading is done.

4
Reviewed on every job

Teachers approve the work before payout and rate every Grade Angel. Teachers stay anonymous to Grade Angels.

A fair question

Why pay a person when AI grading is free?

AI tools are fast, and we are not against them. Our Grade Angels may use them for the routine parts. But a score is not the same as feedback, and your students can tell the difference.

Free AI gradingA Grade Angel
Essays and open responsesScores against patterns and can miss the point a student was trying to makeA real educator reads for meaning, effort, and growth
Feedback students readComments that often read as genericPersonal notes in the tone you ask for
Your rubric and keyFollowed literallyFollowed with judgment, and questions come to you in chat
Messy handwriting and scansOften misreadRead by a person
Student privacyWork may be uploaded to a company that trains on itBackground checked graders under a signed confidentiality agreement
Who is accountableSoftware, not a personA reviewed Grade Angel, and you approve before anyone is paid

Use AI for what it does well. Send us the work that needs a human eye.
